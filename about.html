About Me

On any given weekend, you can find me fly fishing in quiet streams, refining my focus on the golf course, or pacing myself during long runs through the city's parks. These pursuits aren't just hobbies; they are where I challenge my discipline, practice patience, and find a grounded appreciation for nature away from the urban bustle.

Whether analyzing historical texts or exploring museums, I treat history as a puzzle that helps me understand our current world—fueling my passion for critical thinking and research.

Ultimately, I value accountability, resilience, and hands-on learning. Whether I am earning a tactical trade certification, leading a team initiative, or setting academic benchmarks, I believe in showing up fully. I thrive on setting ambitious goals and putting in the consistent, day-to-day work required to achieve them.
