About Me

I am a dedicated student and leader driven by a deep curiosity for history and a commitment to community service. My journey has been defined by the pursuit of excellence, whether leading a service project as an Eagle Scout or analyzing historical events.

Growing up with a fishing rod in one hand and a history book in the other, I learned the value of patience and perspective. Serving as Head Acolyte and playing on the varsity golf team taught me reliability, discipline, and mental fortitude.

My passions lie in historical preservation and outdoor activities. My career goal is to work in cultural heritage, historical research, or policy informed by historical data. I strive to live by my motto: "Leave it better than you found it."
